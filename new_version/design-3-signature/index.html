    ( 08 — FAQs )

    Common questions.

    Still have a question? Talk to our team and we will help you out.

    What makes Morphora Spaces different from other office interior companies?

    We go beyond looks. Our team combines creative interior design with smart space planning to deliver offices that work well, match your brand and are ready for the future — all within 60 days.

    How long does it take to design and build an office?

    We deliver complete office projects in 60 days. Design, buying materials and construction are all planned together, so there is very little downtime for your business.

    Do you work outside Mumbai and Thane?

    Yes. We work with clients across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and other major cities in India. Our team manages projects on-site or remotely, depending on the location and size.

    What is included in your Design & Build service?

    Everything from the first idea, 3D views and layout planning to electrical and civil work, furniture buying and final execution — one team handles it all for you.

    Can you help us find the right office space first?

    Yes. Our Real Estate Advisory team helps you find, check and finalise the right office location, so the space suits your business before we start designing.

    What kind of businesses do you work with?

    Corporate offices, startups, co-working spaces and professional firms. Every project is planned around the company’s goals, work culture and industry needs.
