Project management, done right.

Our Project Management Consultancy (PMC) makes sure your project is delivered quickly, clearly and under control — even when we are not the designers.

Get a free consultation
( 01 — The service )

You stay in control, we stay on site.

Our PMC team makes sure your project is delivered accurately, on time, on budget and without surprises.

We coordinate vendors, check quality and keep the work moving — so you stay in control without having to be on site every day.

( 02 — Why choose us )

What you gain.

  1. 01

    The build matches the design

    What gets built on site matches the approved drawings — no changes or misunderstandings.

  2. 02

    Checked bills

    Every vendor bill is checked against the work done on site, so you only pay for what is delivered.

  3. 03

    Snag-free handover

    We find and fix problems before handover, so you don’t inherit unfinished or faulty work.

  4. 04

    Milestone tracking

    The timeline is split into clear milestones, so progress is easy to measure and track.

  5. 05

    Engineers on site

    Qualified PMC engineers supervise the site every day to make sure the work is technically right.

  6. 06

    Support after handover

    We help with documents, warranty checks and any fixes or vendor closure after handover.

( 03 — Our process )

A clear system that works.

01

Site study

We visit the site and talk to your team to understand your vision, space limits, technical needs and timeline.

02

Planning & costing

We prepare layout options, a detailed bill of quantities (BOQ) and vendor sourcing with clear costs.

03

Work monitoring

We coordinate contractors and check quality on site. You track progress through live dashboards and reports.

04

Final handover

We hand over the project with user guides, maintenance schedules and support for the long term.
