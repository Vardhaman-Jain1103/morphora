Homepage redesign · 3 directions

Three directions for a new look.

Each concept is a working, responsive homepage built with Morphora’s own project photography. Open each one and scroll through. Most of the motion only shows on scroll.

Direction 01

Refined

The current website, elevated. Same structure and content, with new type, a cleaner palette and motion throughout.

  • DM Serif Display + Outfit typography
  • Hero slideshow with animated text and a rotating 60-day badge
  • Scroll reveals, counters, parallax and a process line that fills as you scroll
  • Draggable portfolio carousel and a partner logo marquee

Direction 02

Reimagined

Far less text and far more value. Built around what a business owner wants to know: how long it takes, what it costs, and what you have built before.

  • Interactive 60-day timeline you can drag
  • Fit-out cost estimator (area × finish level)
  • Office zones, sectors served and city-filtered projects
  • Callback form, services cards and a short FAQ
Change level: MediumOpen →
Direction 03

Signature

An editorial, architecture-studio feel that positions Morphora as a premium workplace brand rather than a vendor.

  • 60-day preloader, custom cursor, blueprint grid
  • Interactive office floor plan, zone by zone
  • Scroll-expanding hero image and a horizontal project gallery
  • Stacked process cards and a hover-reveal services list
